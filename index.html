Who we are

Why “Castle Creek”?

With a love for growing vegetables and meeting people, a pick-your-own farm seemed like a good place for both.

Castle
From Newcastle, the town where we are located.
Creek
Because Graham Creek runs through our farm.

Directions in Google Maps
From TorontoHighway 401 eastbound
  1. Take exit 436 for Highway 35/115 toward Lindsay/Peterborough.
  2. After 1.8 km, take the exit toward Newcastle.
  3. After 400 m, keep right at the fork, following signs for Newcastle/Durham Regional Highway 2 East, and merge onto Highway 2 East.
  4. Drive 3.4 km through Newcastle. Farrow Avenue is just before the “hole-in-the-wall” train bridge. Turn right onto Farrow Avenue.
  5. After about 300 m, the farm entrance is on your right.
From KingstonHighway 401 westbound
  1. Take exit 448 for Regional Road 18/Newtonville Road toward Newtonville, and turn right onto Regional Road 18.
  2. After 700 m, turn left onto Durham Regional Highway 2.
  3. After 6.8 km you pass through the “hole-in-the-wall” train bridge. Farrow Avenue is just past it.
  4. Turn left onto Farrow Avenue. After about 300 m, the farm entrance is on your right.
